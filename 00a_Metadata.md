## Overall Description

This dataset represents 25 hippocampal samples from female animals collected over three years (2017–2019) during different population cycle phases (Peak, Low, Decline).
All samples were sequenced in April 2024 using Illumina NovaSeq 25B (150 bp paired-end reads).
